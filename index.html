    Quick reference

    • MODE → 1 COMP · 2 CMPLX · 3 BASE · 4 SD · 5 REG · 6 PRGM
    • SHIFT MODE (SETUP) → six screens: angle unit, Fix/Sci/Norm, a b/c ÷ d/c, a+bi ÷ r∠θ, FreqOn/FreqOff, contrast. Use ◀ ▶ to page.
    • SHIFT 7 (CONST) → ten pages of four scientific constants.
    • FMLA → type a two-digit formula number 01–23, or scroll with ▼ ▲, then EXE. SHIFT FMLA (LOOK) shows the formula itself.
    • Prog → P1–P4 of group 1 (the small number on the left); ◀ ▶ step to groups 2 and 3: the twelve built-in programs in your order. Rank them in Programs (on a phone, hold the solar panel). In PRGM mode Prog opens EDIT / RUN / DEL, where ◀ ▶ change bank.
    • In BASE mode: x² = DEC, ^ = HEX, log = BIN, ln = OCT, and x⁻¹ opens the LOGIC menu.
    • In SD/REG: M+ is DT, SHIFT M+ is CL, SHIFT 1 is S-SUM, SHIFT 2 is S-VAR, and ▼ browses the entered samples.
    • Complex numbers: press MODE then 2; the display shows CMPLX. In CMPLX the ENG key types i directly (ALPHA ENG also works). SHIFT EXE switches Re⇔Im. The mode chip under the keypad opens the same menu.
    • In CMPLX: ENG is i, SHIFT (-) is ∠, SHIFT EXE is Re⇔Im, SHIFT ( is arg(, SHIFT ) is Abs(, SHIFT , is Conjg(, SHIFT + is ▶r∠θ and SHIFT − is ▶a+bi. A complex result shows its real part first with R⇔I lit; Re⇔Im shows the imaginary part (i) or, in r∠θ format, the angle (∠).

    Behaviour follows the published fx-50F PLUS / fx-50FH II user's guide: the same six modes, 10+2 digit display, 15-digit internal precision, 99-byte input line, 680 bytes of program memory across four areas (per bank in this edition, which adds any number of banks), 23 built-in formulas and 40 CODATA-2000 constants. The shell is the generated high-resolution reconstruction; the display and every key are live.
